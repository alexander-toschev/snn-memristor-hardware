## Outcome

The 32-tap gate passed in all three replicates. Sign agreement (descriptive) was 31/32, 28/32, 29/32; median mapped-cell error/maxabs was 0.0644, 0.0994, 0.0900 (gate <=0.35); zero-noise MVM/readback max error per active tap/maxabs was 0.00602, 0.00729, 0.00370 (gate <=0.05); held-out validation r was 0.9938, 0.9917, 0.9953 (gate >=0.8), and RMSE/SD(ideal) was 0.1403, 0.1518, 0.1417 (gate <=0.6).

All nine tiles met their mapped-cell and zero-noise gates: maximum median error/maxabs 0.2214; maximum zero-noise discrepancy/active tap/maxabs 0.02079. Tile sign agreement ranged 0.70–0.96875 and was not an advancement gate. Three-tile **aggregated 75-tap** held-out validation of simulator MVM versus ideal, in replicate order: Pearson r 0.95896 / 0.94063 / 0.96977; RMSE 0.06702 / 0.08038 / 0.05821 against SD(ideal) 0.20845 (ratios 0.322 / 0.386 / 0.279). All three fixed aggregate validation gates passed. This is **v2-only pilot validation**, not reversal of v1's failure or full-network/device evidence. The train-only affine surrogate gave held-out RMSE 0.05948 / 0.08052 / 0.05131. The saved summary also reports ideal, quantized digital target, measured readback, quantized programmed digital, and simulator MVM separately.

There were 14,022 MVM calls (budget 14,500), nine arrays, and approximately 168.20 s elapsed until the runner's offline error (budget 1,800 s). Every call had requested and decoded voltages <=0.300 V and predicted current strictly <15 mA on both measured and actual-resistance views; peak was 1.0794 mA, smallest current margin 13.9206 mA. ADC codes ranged 0–1167 (14-bit full-scale 16383); zero corresponds only to zero input. Inference voltages are distinct from the independently logged programming pulses. The v1 runner's hidden clipping was removed in the frozen v2 runner by passing the scaled 0/0.3-V vector to `HardCore.dot_product`, with reconstruction against DAC-decoded programmed conductance. Dry digital controls and actual zero-noise simulator controls passed.
